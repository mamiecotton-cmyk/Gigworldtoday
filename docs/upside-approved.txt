Upside is a free cash-back app that partners with gig platforms such as Uber and Instacart to help drivers earn cash back on fuel and eligible in-store purchases — including groceries and restaurant orders — at participating merchants. Drivers claim an offer in the Upside app, pay as usual, and receive cash back that helps offset driving and operating costs. Availability and eligible categories vary by location and by the offers shown in the app.
